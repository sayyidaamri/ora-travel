      ORA Travel Privacy Notice

      Dear ORA Travel guest,

      ORA Travel Ltd. ("ORA Travel") respects your privacy. In line with applicable personal-data protection law, this notice explains how we collect, process and use your personal information.

      1. Why we collect your data

      When you register on our website, use online chat, send an enquiry or booking form, or speak to one of our planners, we collect personal data to serve you, manage client records, provide after-sales support, and send marketing, newsletters and text messages, as well as for the other uses described in our privacy policy.

      2. What we collect

      Your name, contact details (including but not limited to phone, e-mail and address), nationality, employer, job title and any other data that can identify you directly or indirectly.

      3. Period, place, recipients and method of use

      (1) Period: for as long as ORA Travel operates, or as long as the law requires us to keep records.
      (2) Place: in the countries where we operate, the countries of recipients you agree to, and any successor company after a merger, split or transfer of business.
      (3) Recipients: ORA Travel, its affiliates, contractors acting on our behalf, our suppliers and partners, companies or organisations that ask us to run events, judicial and government authorities with legal powers of investigation, and other recipients of international data transfers not restricted by the competent authority.
      (4) Method: by phone, text message, e-mail, paper or any other appropriate technology of the time.

      4. Your rights

      (1) To ask to see or receive a copy of your data (we may charge a reasonable fee).
      (2) To ask us to complete or correct your data.
      (3) To ask us to delete, stop processing or stop using your data once the purpose no longer applies, unless we need it to perform our duties or business.

      5. If you prefer not to share your data

      If you do not want to provide your personal data for the purposes above, ORA Travel may decide whether to process your order or enquiry, or whether you can use or join any service or activity offered by ORA Travel or its partners. Please contact our team with any questions.

      6. Changes to this notice

      ORA Travel may update this notice at any time and will publish the updated version in an appropriate place on our website. You can review it here whenever you like.
