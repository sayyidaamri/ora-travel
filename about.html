      ORA Travel: our story

      Discovery

      Since1980‘s

      Past
      Born
      In the early 1980s ORA Travel began almost by accident, booking crew flights between Jakarta and the busy fishing harbours of Cape Town and Port Louis. Those long-haul tickets taught us African travel inside out, and soon we opened our own desks in Johannesburg, Cape Town, Durban and Bloemfontein: the most complete South Africa network in the region.

      The1990‘s

      The 90s
      Travel
      As incomes grew and holidays became part of everyday life, we saw how hungry people were to see the world. ORA opened its group-tour wholesale desk and built new series to Europe, Australia and New Zealand, North America, Southeast Asia, Japan and, of course, our beloved South Africa.

      Time to2000‘s

      21st century
      Rewards
      Companies began to see incentive travel (INCENTIVE TOURS) as a serious way to thank their best people. ORA set up a dedicated incentive team, obsessed with creating a fresh, original proposal for every client. Today many firms trust us with their reward trips each year, and keep coming back.

      Reward travel INCENTIVE TOURS

      An unforgettable incentive trip is never born in a single moment. We believe each one is built, step by step, from the same pieces.
      Let us show you how careful planning and a devoted team turn a simple reward into a memory that stays with your people for years.

      An idea

      Every perfect journey starts with an idea, a phone call or an email. This is where we listen: tell us what you hope the trip will achieve, and share the values and goals that drive your company. The trip should attract, motivate and reward the people who matter most, and strengthen how they feel about your brand. The more we learn, the more precisely we can tailor a programme no ordinary holiday could offer. So contact us today, by phone, online or face to face, whichever suits you best.
      1

      Creative spark

      This is where ORA's own thinking comes in, turning your idea into a set of real options. Our incentive specialists have run programmes on every continent, and we use that experience to build, piece by piece, journeys that are creative, surprising and impossible to refuse. Take your time to weigh them up; we will adjust every detail as your plans evolve. It is your one-of-a-kind trip, and our job is to help you deliver the reward your team deserves.
      2

      Unique packaging

      Once the direction is agreed, your guests need to feel how much they are valued. We care about every touchpoint: invitation decks, travel booklets, luggage tags, gala dinners and even the wrapping on the welcome gifts. Inside and out, we make every element work harder so each participant senses the value of the reward. Whatever the request, we are ready to help, making sure every guest comes home inspired and closer to the team.
      3

      Flawless operation

      Every project has its own dedicated team, so each trip runs like clockwork. We make sure everything is in place, because a smooth journey and the safety of your guests are the two things we care about most. Each year we look after many corporate programmes and thousands of travellers in every corner of the world. Relax and let our specialists handle the planning, the partners and every detail in between.
      4

      The big reveal

      This is the most exciting part, when all our shared work comes to life. Perhaps you are flying by helicopter over the South African savanna to watch wildlife from above, teeing off on the 18th hole at the edge of a canyon, racing buggies across the dunes or rising at dawn among hundreds of hot-air balloons. These are memories money alone cannot buy, and ORA is right beside you to make sure it all goes perfectly.
      5

      What do you get?

      This is why ORA does incentive travel: a well-crafted reward recognises your best people, lifts productivity and pride at work, and over time lowers the cost of keeping great talent. You will be surprised how far the impact of one extraordinary trip can reach, which is exactly why it is so hard to resist.
