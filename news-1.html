      A table for forty: how we plan incentive gala dinners abroad

      From private wine cellars to desert camps, the secret to a memorable gala is in the details nobody notices.

      The secret to a memorable gala is in the details nobody notices.

      Forty guests, one evening, and a single chance to make it unforgettable. Gala dinners are the heart of most incentive programmes, and our events team plans them months in advance. Here is a look behind the scenes.

       

      Choosing the venue

      A private wine cellar, a desert camp or a rooftop above the city — the venue sets the mood before anyone sits down. We visit every option in person before we propose it.

       

      The menu

      We work with chefs to build a menu around local produce, then adapt it for every dietary need on the guest list. Nobody should feel like an afterthought.

       

      Music and moments

      A string quartet for arrival, a local performance between courses and a short awards ceremony before dessert. Timing is everything.

       

      Getting everyone home

      The evening ends with transfers waiting at the door. The final detail is the one guests never think about — and that is exactly the point.
